The Box Model

The Box Model is how content is visually displayed and structured on a website. This means that EVERYTHING you see is inside of a box!

There are 4 properties in the Box Model:

Content

Padding

Border

Margin

Content

  • The width and height size of a piece of content
    • Examples: images, text, video, etc

Padding

  • The space in between the content and the edges of the box (borders)
  • Visualize it like mailing an item in a package
    • You have a cardboard box of a specific size (certain length, certain width)
    • The item to mail is your content (Ex: Wireless Earbuds)
    • Padding (if you choose to add it) fills in the space inside of the cardboard box, around the wireless earbuds (content)

Border

  • It's pretty much what it sounds like
  • In CSS, the main three things you can change is the border color, style and thickness
    • Examples: Dotted, Solid, 3px, 10px, and any other color you can think of

Margin

  • A space you specify that determine how far away OTHER elements on a page are
